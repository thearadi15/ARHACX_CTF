# 📜 Preamble's Prefix

**Category:** Crypto  
**Difficulty:** EASY  
**Points:** 300  

## Description
Sentinel,

On 26th January 1950, our nation's identity was etched into history. The National Archive's integrity system relies on the core pillars of our constitution to secure its most sensitive records.

We have intercepted a sequence of four integrity hashes from a secure transmission (`national_archive.pcap`). Intelligence indicates that these were generated by combining our Foundational Values with the Nature of our Republic.

The values are hidden within the visual ledger (`preamble.jpg`). You must find the four missing descriptors that define our Republic to verify the signatures.

The strength of the Union is the sum of its parts.

## Files Provided
- `preamble.jpg`: A high-resolution scan of the Preamble.
- `national_archive.pcap`: Captured network traffic containing four integrity hashes.
- `founding_principles.txt`: Wordlist for cracking the hashes.

## Notes
- **Metadata Matters:** The image holds more than just a visual message.
- **Hash Logic:** The salt (prefix) comes before the word. Format: `SALTword` (e.g., `JUSTICEsovereign`).
- **Wordlist:** Focus on the descriptors of the Republic found within the Preamble text itself.
- **Flag Format:** `CTF{Word1_Word2_Word3_Word4}`
