Challenge Description 

Challenge Name: Tricolor Cipher
Category: Steganography
Difficulty: Hard / Final Round

Story:

As the nation prepares to celebrate the 26th of January, a sealed digital archive is recovered from a forgotten system once used for confidential communications during turbulent times. The archive is said to contain a message of great significance — a message that was never meant to be read easily.

At the center lies a peculiar image bearing the face of a monkey, symbolizing mischief, illusion, and the idea that what is visible cannot always be trusted. The truth is present, yet veiled, protected by layers that distort and disguise its real form.

Surrounding it are four other images, each linked to powerful and controversial figures from the era of India’s struggle for independence — Mahatma Gandhi, Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose, Jawaharlal Nehru, and Nathuram Godse. History remembers them in very different lights, yet here they are bound together by a common secret, each carrying a fragment of something larger.

Not every fragment speaks honestly.
Not every pattern is what it first appears to be.
Some pieces have been altered by time.
Some are hidden deeper than others.
Some exist only when viewed from the right perspective.

This is not a puzzle of a single file, but of unity.
Not of a single truth, but of reconstruction.

Only those who can look beyond surface appearances, question what seems complete, and patiently piece together what history has scattered will be able to uncover the concealed message — and with it, the flag that marks the end of the journey.
